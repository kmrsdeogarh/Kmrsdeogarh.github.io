About the School

Learning with purpose, values and opportunity

KMRS Deogarh provides a supportive residential learning environment for students and aims to develop academic ability, confidence, discipline and responsible citizenship.

🎓

Academic Focus

Structured learning from middle school through senior secondary level, with emphasis on strong fundamentals and preparation for higher education.

🏫

Residential Education

A campus-based environment designed to support learning, routines, co-curricular activities and student development.

🌱

Holistic Development

Academic learning is complemented by sports, cultural activities, life skills, leadership and value-based education.

Affiliation Requirements

CBSE requirements cover land, physical infrastructure, prescribed documents, website information, qualified staff and inspection/verification.

Prepare

Check applicable CBSE Affiliation Bye-Laws and required infrastructure.

Documents

Prepare the prescribed government/education authority certificate and affidavit.

Apply

Submit the relevant application through the SARAS portal.

Inspection

School infrastructure and facilities are subject to the prescribed inspection process.

Decision

After compliance and inspection, CBSE processes the application as per its rules.
