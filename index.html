About Our School

Kalinga Model Residential School (KMRS), Deogarh is a residential school under the ST & SC Development Department, Government of Odisha. The school is committed to quality education, holistic development, discipline and value-based learning.

The school campus at Sunamunda provides an environment for academic learning, co-curricular activities, sports and student development.

Principal's Message

“Our aim is to provide quality education, create a positive learning environment and help every student discover their true potential.”

Arabinda Pradhan
Principal, KMRS Deogarh

Academics

Classes VI–VIII

Foundation learning with languages, mathematics, science, social science and co-curricular development.

Classes IX–X

Secondary education with focused subject learning and preparation for board-level studies.

Classes XI–XII

Senior secondary education with subject-focused learning and preparation for higher studies.
